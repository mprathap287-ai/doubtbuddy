FAQ

Questions parents ask.

Is DoubtBuddy an AI bot? +
No. We use AI internally to prepare answers quickly — but every response your child receives comes from a real human who has reviewed and humanized it. You will always feel a warm, personal touch.
Which classes and subjects are covered? +
We currently support Class 6, 7, and 8 students for Maths and Science (both plans). The Priority plan also covers all other subjects including English, Social Science, and Kannada.
What are the support timings? +
Standard plan: 6 PM – 8 PM. Priority plan: 6 PM – 10 PM. We are offline during school hours. Away messages will let your child know when we are back.
Will you just give my child the answers? +
Never. Our goal is guided learning — not answer delivery. We explain concepts, walk through steps, and encourage your child to attempt the solution. The child must do the work.
Is this a replacement for tuition? +
No — think of us as an evening study companion. If your child already has tuition, we fill the gaps at night. Many families use both.
